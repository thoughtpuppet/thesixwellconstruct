01 / Place

Reading the names

Guardian Studios is the artist-studio building within the wider Echo Street West development. Echo Contemporary Art is the gallery housed inside Guardian Studios; it is not an alternate name for the whole studio building.0107

Current Echo Contemporary pages give the studio and gallery address as 785 Echo Street NW. Echo Street West's campus pages use 765 Echo Street NW in their shared footer. This dossier therefore uses 785 for the specific place and preserves 765 as the broader campus address rather than treating one as a silent correction of the other.0106

The address sits within Echo Street West, a 19-acre mixed-use project in Atlanta's English Avenue area. RIOS describes its plan as a network of offices, housing, retail, artist and maker workshops, event spaces, trails, and landscape organized around the site's industrial and railroad context.08

02 / Fabric

Industrial inheritance

Echo Street West identifies Guardian Studios as an early-1900s warehouse adapted for a new artist community. The developer also says the names Guardian Studios and neighboring Guardian Works refer to the site's former Guardian Company complex and that the reuse retained industrial architecture.0102

A Guardian Works venue guide describes the adjacent event building as a renovated 1908 structure with histories as a chemical company and perfumery. That marketing record does not establish that every part of the former complex—including the present Guardian Studios building—shared the same construction date or sequence of industrial uses.11

RIOS frames the larger development through the industrial corridor and former railroad alignment, while its 2023 completion date refers to the design project it presents—not proof that every proposed phase or use across Echo Street West was finished that year.0809

03 / Formation

Building the studio community

In June 2021, Lincoln Property Company described a first phase of 14 rentable art studios measuring 200 to 500 square feet, a 3,000-square-foot open gallery, and murals. The same announcement projected another 26 studios and named Black Women in Visual Arts as the organization tapped to curate and introduce the program.02

By November 2021, Urbanize Atlanta reported the first 14 studios open and leased. It described a revised second-phase count of 17 additional artist spaces plus a ceramics studio, with new spaces ranging from 240 to more than 1,000 square feet. The difference from the earlier 26-studio projection records a changing plan, not two simultaneously verified final counts.03

Later reporting says Black Women in Visual Arts managed the studios during the first year. Jessica Helfrecht said she joined Lincoln's team in July 2022 after BWVA chose not to renew its contract; she became Guardian Studios' studio manager while creating and owning Echo Contemporary Art.04

04 / Gallery

Echo Contemporary Art

Echo Contemporary Art held its grand opening inside Guardian Studios on August 19, 2022. The announcement described it as a new gallery for emerging and mid-career Southeastern artists and named the artists in its inaugural exhibition.05

The gallery's current description continues that focus through curated month-long exhibitions and rentals for schools, MFA students, and curators. Its current events listing documents ongoing exhibitions and the fifth annual Guardian Studios Artists exhibition in 2026.0610

By 2024, Rough Draft Atlanta reported that Echo had presented more than 500 artists in 30 exhibitions during its first two years. The source attributes that count to Helfrecht and documents the gallery's expanding use of guest curators, open calls, and college exhibitions.04

05 / Firsthand

A lived studio thread, 2023–2025

Saiel Dauhn Solehman entered Studio 30 around April 29, 2023. The 241-square-foot workspace rented for $588.04 per month, and she used it through approximately May 31, 2023.13

Around June 1, 2023, Solehman moved directly into the larger Studio 20. The 380-square-foot workspace rented for $927.70 per month, and this second phase continued until approximately January 7, 2025.13

06 / Present

An active studio and gallery building

Guardian Studios' current official page says the building supports up to 31 studios and places Echo Contemporary Art in a 3,000-square-foot gallery. Echo's rental page says more than 40 creatives work from the building across painting, photography, fashion, sculpture, murals, art therapy, styling, and ceramics.0107

The published size range has changed with inventory and time. The launch page describes studios from 200 to 1,000 square feet; the current page lists rentable spaces from 163 to 626 square feet. Neither range should be substituted for the exact firsthand dimensions of Solehman's former Studio 30 and Studio 20.01

Urbanize reported all 31 studios fully leased in 2023, while Echo's 2026 schedule documents continuing exhibitions and an annual Guardian Studios artist show. Those sources establish activity at their respective dates; they do not prove a constant occupancy rate across every later month.0910
