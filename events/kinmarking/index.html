the series

Marks carried forward.

KINMARKING is a participatory memory, archive, and tattoo practice exploring how photographs, documents, objects, stories, inherited symbols, and family histories may be interpreted as marks carried in the skin.

the interval

Every four months.

Each edition holds its own theme, archival questions, participating archivist, and tattoo possibilities while remaining connected to the wider KINMARKING practice.
