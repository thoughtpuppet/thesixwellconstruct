SOLEHMAN’S NEW YEAR :00

Soft launch + gathering.

The Six.Well Construct presents SNY00.
November 6, 2026 · 7–9 p.m.
364 Nelson Street SW.

???

SNY00 is the soft launch of SOLEHMAN’S NEW YEAR: an annual exposition by artist Saiel Dauhn Solehman.

The evening includes a short Signal & Symbol activity and introductions to GREEN[field] and KINMARKING. This is an opportunity to get to know the projects and explore a closer look at the questions influencing the work before they become public.

You’ve been invited to see what’s being cultivated, ask questions, share your thoughts, and be part of the conversation.

KINMARKING 00

KINMARKING is an artistic research project in development.

SIGNAL & SYMBOL 00

Signal ’n Symbol is a play on the classic Sip ’n Paint, but with an investigative twist.

GREEN[FIELD] 00

GREEN[field] is a showcase for the various rock collections cultivated amid day jobs and disbelonging, sustained by untamed curiosity and courage.

Documentation

This gathering will be documented by Tyrone Hawk.
