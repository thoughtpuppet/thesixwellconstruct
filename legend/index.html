How to read it

One language. Many forms.

The Legend does not assign one permanent definition to every mark. It documents a symbol's core idea, the meanings it has gathered, how its form changes, and where it appears throughout the Construct.

Symbol

The mark, figure, gesture, shape, character, or recurring visual structure.

02

Meaning

03

Variations

How the symbol mutates, combines with others, or changes through context.

04

Appearances

The tattoos, paintings, films, garments, writings, objects, and spaces it inhabits.
