Paper sign-in reconciliation.

Guests sign the paper sheet at the door. Later, enter each sheet and row reference here, such as “sheet 1 / row 4”. Choose the matching RSVP or record a walk-in. Saving the same row updates it. This records a transcription, not a digital arrival time.

RSVP plans.

These are planned headcounts. A confirmed RSVP is not proof of attendance. Food notes are private planning information; do not copy them to the door sheet or public archive.

Reconciled attendance.

Walk-ins count only toward actual attendance. Neither RSVP nor paper attendance grants likeness, quote, marketing, or publication permission.
