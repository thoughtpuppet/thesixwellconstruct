Inquiry & Application Forms

Public pages where clients submit inquiries before any approval or booking link exists. On success they redirect to "Inquiry Received" below; on failure they show an inline error and let the client retry. "Open Live Page" links are the real forms - submitting them creates real inquiries. The consultation and build pages also support ?preview=1, which loads dummy calendar windows and disables the Square checkout.
