## Interpretive constraint

These sheets document block conditions and the absence of the present
multi-story brick warehouse in 1911. Historic street numbering has not yet
been matched conclusively to the current parcel, so the maps must not be used
to claim that a named household or business occupied the exact footprint of
today's building.

The full 1922 Atlanta city directory is also public domain, but its scan is
roughly 204 MB and the relevant leaf has not been confirmed. It remains linked
externally rather than being copied or excerpted here.

National Register photographs, government PDFs containing third-party work,
trade publications, journalism, and organization websites remain link-only
sources unless a specific reuse license or permission is obtained.
