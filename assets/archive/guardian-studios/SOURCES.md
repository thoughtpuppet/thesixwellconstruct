# Guardian Studios at Echo Street West — research and visual-source manifest

Research cutoff: 2026-08-30

This folder currently contains no copied photographs, maps, lease records,
payment records, or other documentary attachments. The public Guardian Studios
place dossier is deliberately text-led. Strong developer, gallery, design-team,
and editorial photography remains available through external source links, but
no reviewed source carried an open reuse license suitable for a local public
copy.

## Place and institutional hierarchy

- Guardian Studios is the canonical artist-studio place within the wider Echo
  Street West campus.
- Echo Contemporary Art is the gallery housed inside Guardian Studios. It is
  not registered as a second place for the same physical studio address.
- Echo Contemporary's current pages publish `785 Echo Street NW`; Echo Street
  West's shared campus footer publishes `765 Echo Street NW`. The dossier keeps
  the specific-place and campus addresses distinct.

## Industrial claim boundary

Echo Street West describes Guardian Studios as an early-1900s warehouse and
says the Guardian Studios and Guardian Works names refer to the former Guardian
Company complex. A Guardian Works marketing guide separately describes the
adjacent event building as a renovated 1908 former chemical-company and
perfumery structure. Until a building-specific Sanborn, directory, deed, or
environmental record is matched, the 1908 date and chemical/perfumery sequence
must not be assigned to the present Guardian Studios building as established
fact.

No lease, payment record, financial document, photograph, or other private
attachment was requested or supplied. The statement is sufficient as a
firsthand source. It does not establish total rent paid, deposits, lease terms,
