About the house

Marks with meaning.

Art.Pill Tattoo House is the tattoo practice of Saiel Dauhn Solehman: a space where the body becomes a site for symbolism, memory, collaboration, and transformation.

The practice

Built around intention.

The work moves between original flash, custom review, symbolic systems, and special projects. Each path begins with the idea and the person carrying it, then finds the form that belongs on the body.

Art.Pill sits inside the larger Six.Well Construct while keeping its own room, rhythm, and record. The tattoo is treated as both a personal object and a living part of an evolving visual language.

Process & philosophy

The body changes the image.

For me, tattooing is not just placing an image on skin. It is a collaboration between a person, a symbol, a body, and time. Meaning begins before the appointment and keeps changing after the piece leaves the studio.

01

Listen

The process begins with what the person is carrying: an idea, memory, tension, transformation, or image they cannot leave alone.

02

Translate

I look for the visual structure beneath the request and translate it through composition, symbolism, placement, and the language of the body.

03

Make together

The design becomes specific through conversation. Custom work is not a one-way commission; the final form is shaped through exchange.

04

Let it live

A tattoo changes with movement, healing, memory, and the life around it. The finished piece is a beginning rather than a fixed endpoint.

Meaning in the work

Symbols travel.

Some forms return throughout my work, but they do not belong only to tattooing. They move between skin, paintings, garments, film, writing, objects, and rooms across the Construct.

The Legend holds the broader language behind those recurring symbols. Individual artwork records can then explain how that language changes inside one particular piece.
