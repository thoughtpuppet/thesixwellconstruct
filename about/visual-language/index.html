Sources of visual language.

The construct's visual language is not a separate brand skin. It comes from the places where the work is already making symbols: tattooing, painting, objects, garments, and the Six.Well system that holds them together.

visual language

Where the look comes from.

The visible world of the construct comes from repeated source systems, not decoration. It carries the pressure of marks, paintings, garments, labels, ritual objects, and the operating structure that keeps them related.

art.pill

art.pill brings the mark, the body, the threshold, and the client-facing ritual. Its influence shows up in symbolic linework, flash logic, protection, consent, aftercare, and the seriousness of a mark that has to live on someone.

Painting / ThoughtPuppet

Painting is the image root. ThoughtPuppet holds the studies, color relationships, subconscious figures, object memory, and visual vocabulary that can later become tattoos, garments, films, archive records, or public symbols.

Six.Well

Six.Well turns the source language into a system: navigation, hierarchy, clothing, editions, labels, tools, and repeatable structures. It makes the visual world operational without flattening it into a generic identity.
