orientation

What this is.

The Six.Well Construct is the full operating architecture. It holds named nodes, creative mediums, public entry points, private logic, and the archive record that helps people find the right door.

The construct

The system that lets the work stay operational, poetic, commercial, private, public, and alive at the same time.

Nodes

Named houses and project rooms: art.pill Tattoo House, ThoughtPuppet, Sloth99, Six.Well Clothing, Mindful Darkness, and The Seventh Room.

Mediums

The forms the work moves through: tattoo, painting, film, writing, clothing, and space or interior.

Archive

The record layer: works, process, releases, events, documents, and experiments that still speak after the active room changes.

About

origin

Who made it.

Saiel Dauhn Solehman is an interdisciplinary artist and founder working across tattooing, painting, systems, objects, writing, film, sound, and event-based forms.

Founder statement

I build systems because the work keeps arriving in more than one form. The construct is the room I made so the forms could stop apologizing for knowing each other.

Method

Observe the charge, choose the node or medium, make the work, document the residue, and let the result alter the system.

Discipline stack

Tattooing, painting, film, writing, design, hosting, commerce, archive, and system architecture.
