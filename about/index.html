orientation

What this is.

The Six.Well Construct is the creative ecosystem of Saiel Dauhn Solehman, where inquiry, work,  resources, and history support one another. The map connects creative practices, commerce, and shared systems, each of which can be a starting point for ideation. Ideas can diverge to expand their possibilities, associations, and contexts, and converge to become more clearly defined through those contexts and connections. Ideation, thought, and execution develop ideas across these parts through different mediums and modes; making and encountering the work can open further possibilities and deepen understanding. A painting, for example, can lead to clothing, a tattoo, or an exhibition. Following its symbols across those contexts helps trace how their meanings develop. The Archive keeps the material and history of this development connected.

The construct

The system that keeps the work operational and the structure that anchors the philosophy informing its brands, modes, and mediums. An idea can move between projects and forms while keeping its connections to the work, people, and material that shaped it.

Modes

The methods, styles, and sensory channels through which work is developed, experienced, or communicated: visual, auditory, tactile, narrative, or participatory. Modes can shape ideation, thought, and execution. One work can involve several modes, and the same mode can operate across different parts of the construct.

Mediums

The physical materials and tools used to create work: paint, canvas, wood, fabric, thread, tattoo ink, needles, cameras, or instruments. Their properties, possibilities, and constraints can generate ideas and shape making. One practice can use several mediums, and a medium can be used across different parts of the construct.

Creative identities

The names under which creative practices develop and share their work. These include art.pill Tattoo House, ThoughtPuppet, Sloth99, Six.Well Clothing, MILOWALKSONWATER, Mindful Darkness, The Seventh Room, and THE SOLEHMAN LETTERS. Their practices can work through several modes and mediums while retaining their own purpose and relationships within the construct.

Archive

The connected record of inception, creation, presentation, and maintenance. Sources, studies, process material, objects, releases, events, and later changes can stay linked to the work they belong to. Collecting and connecting that material can reveal relationships, deepen understanding, and inform future work.

Legend

The shared visual vocabulary of the construct. A symbol record holds its documented meanings, variations, and appearances, giving someone a way to follow the same symbol through paintings, tattoos, clothing, and other works. Exploring those contexts can deepen understanding of the symbol and the works containing it.

practices / histories

Creative identities.

The names under which creative practices develop and share their work. Their practices can work through several modes and mediums while retaining their own purpose and relationships within the construct. Published Archive timelines offer a deeper look at their development.

art.pill Tattoo House

Tattooing as listening, interpretation, and collaborative mark-making. Images and symbols meet a person's intentions and the body that will carry them.

ThoughtPuppet

Painting, images, objects, and studies through which visual inquiry develops. The work can retain its own significance while contributing imagery and ideas to other practices.

Six.Well Clothing

Garments and objects as an ongoing artistic practice and collective culture. Wearing and using the work creates further encounters with its imagery and ideas.

Sloth99

Moving images, narrative, and documentation. Film offers ways to develop ideas through sequences, duration, and the relationships between image and sound.

MILOWALKSONWATER

The music and sound identity within the construct. Its place in the wider practice continues to develop through making and listening.

Mindful Darkness

Sustained reflection and written inquiry. WRKNG makes room for unfinished thinking, allowing questions to remain open as they develop.

THE SOLEHMAN LETTERS

Direct correspondence that brings writing into an ongoing relationship with its readers.

The Seventh Room

An evolving practice of space, interiors, hosting, and atmosphere. The room itself can shape how work is encountered and how people gather.

GREEN[FIELD]

A recurring gathering that brings performers, participants, and audiences into a shared creative space. It develops within the wider Events practice.

artist / founder

Saiel Dauhn Solehman.

Saiel Dauhn Solehman is an Atlanta native and interdisciplinary artist whose practice brings philosophical inquiry and systems thinking into making. The construct holds her creative practices together while preserving their identities, relationships, and histories.
