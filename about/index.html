What this is.

The Six.Well Construct is the full operating solo creative ecosystem of saiel dauhn solehman. Its purpose is to create an environment where ideas and projects exist as interconnected parts of a larger world. Each node supports each other. It holds named nodes, creative mediums, public-facing entry points, world-building logic, and the archive record that helps place it.

The construct

The system that lets the work stay operational, and an anchor of the philosophy that informs all brands, nodes and mediums.

Nodes

Named houses, domains and project containers: art.pill Tattoo House, ThoughtPuppet, Sloth99, Six.Well Clothing, CULT[&SHIFT], MILOWALKSONWATER, Mindful Darkness, The Seventh Room and THE SOLEHMAN LETTERS.

Mediums

The forms the work MANIFESTS as: tattooing, paintings, woodworks, film, animation, writing, clothing and other objects, music and space or interior.

Archive

The record layer: works, process, releases, events, documents, and experiments that still speak after the active room changes.
