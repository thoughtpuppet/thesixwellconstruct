Inquiry.

Submitting an inquiry is not a booking. It begins a review process — not every project is accepted. If your project is a good fit, you'll receive a private booking link within 1–2 weeks.

Include as much detail as you can. The more specific you are about what you want and why, the better the review process works for both of us.

What to Include

  • What you want — concept, subject matter, the meaning behind it
  • Where you want it — body placement
  • Approximate size — doesn't need to be exact
  • Flash or custom — if you have a specific flash piece in mind, say so
  • Any visual references — mood and aesthetic, not necessarily the final design
  • Your timeline — when you're hoping to get tattooed
  • Any relevant context — this helps place the work in the right session type

Inquiries are submitted via the Art.Pill booking system. You'll be prompted for your contact information and project details. Response time is 1–2 weeks.
