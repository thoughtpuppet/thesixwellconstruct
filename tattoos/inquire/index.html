Inquiry.

Submitting an inquiry is not a booking. It begins a review process and not every project is accepted. If your project is a good fit, you will receive a private booking link within 1–2 weeks.

Include as much detail as you can. The more specific you are about what you want and why, the better the review process works for both of us.

What to Include

  • What you want: concept, subject matter, and the meaning behind it
  • Where you want it: body placement
  • Approximate size: it does not need to be exact
  • Flash or custom: if you have a specific flash piece in mind, say so
  • Visual references: mood and aesthetic, not necessarily the final design
  • Your timeline: when you are hoping to get tattooed
  • Any relevant context that helps place the work in the right session type

Inquiries are submitted via the Art.Pill booking system. You will be prompted for your contact information and project details. Response time is 1–2 weeks.
