What to prepare

Come in with the right information

Be ready to share the core idea, placement, approximate scale, timing, and any references that clarify atmosphere rather than dictate an exact copy.

What happens next

Project review

Each inquiry is reviewed for fit, complexity, timing, and whether it belongs inside the Art.Pill visual language.

Approval flow

Private booking link after approval

If the piece is approved, you will receive a private booking link and deposit instructions. Public visitors do not book sessions directly from the site.

Good inquiry habits
  • Explain the symbolic or emotional charge of the piece.
  • Note size, placement, and timeline honestly.
  • Use references to communicate tone, not to demand duplication.
  • Mention if the project should be treated as flash, custom, or a larger mythic session.
