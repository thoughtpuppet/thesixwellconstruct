Before You Submit

What to include

The more specific you are about what you want and why, the better the review process works for both of us. Narrative work is welcome, but it is not the only fit here.

Inquiry checklist
  • Core concept, image, or emotional direction
  • Body placement and approximate scale
  • Whether the piece feels like flash or custom work
  • References that clarify atmosphere, not exact duplication
  • Your timing and any relevant context
How Review Works

Review first, booking second

Inquiries are reviewed for fit, complexity, timing, and whether the project belongs inside the Art.Pill visual language. If approved, you receive a separate booking link and deposit instructions.
