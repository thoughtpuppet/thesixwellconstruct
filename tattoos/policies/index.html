Studio & Booking Policies

The studio's Terms & Conditions are expressed below. Please read everything carefully before booking your appointment. Booking an appointment confirms that you have read and understood the studio policies below.


Before booking

Review access, design, and fit.

  • Consultations: Consultations are not mandatory. However, they are available if needed either in person or via Zoom.
  • Age Requirement: Clients must be at least 18 years old with valid ID. No exceptions.
  • Designs: Your design will be shown on the day of your tattoo appointment unless a consultation has been requested, approved, and paid for.
  • Design Direction: Your paid tattoo deposit includes one developed design direction. If I approve a substantially different alternate concept that you request, a separate non-refundable $50 drawing fee is due before work begins on that concept. The fee is not credited toward the tattoo total. Minor refinements to the chosen direction and artist-initiated redraws remain included. Alternate concepts must be requested before the appointment; an unpaid request does not change the booked appointment or the included design direction.
  • Style: Exact copies or duplicates are not offered. I create in my own style. If that doesn't align with your vision, I may not be the right artist for you.
After booking

Deposits, timing, and day-of boundaries.

  • Grace Period: You're given a 15-minute grace period. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment will be canceled and rescheduled, which will require a new deposit. Please make sure you give yourself enough time to find the studio and park to avoid this.
  • Deposits: All deposits are non-refundable and go toward the final cost of your tattoo. You are allowed one reschedule if requested at least 48 hours in advance. Rescheduling requests made within 48 hours of your appointment will require a new deposit.
  • Final Payment: Your deposit goes toward the final cost of your tattoo. At the start of your appointment, after the final design, placement, and session price are confirmed, the remaining balance must be paid before tattooing begins. Tattooing will not begin until payment has been received. Payment options are cash (preferred), Cash App, Apple Pay, or credit/debit card. A 3% processing fee is applied to all digital transactions.
  • Design Changes: Major last-minute changes require rescheduling and a new deposit.
  • No-Show Policy: Failure to show up to your appointment without notice is considered a no-show. No-shows forfeit their deposit and will not be rebooked in the future.
  • Guest Policy: Please notify me prior to your appointment if you'll be having a guest join you.
  • Health & Safety: If you arrive visibly under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or if you are unwell—including active rashes, infections, or open wounds near the tattoo area—I reserve the right to refuse service.
