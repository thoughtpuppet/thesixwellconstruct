Booking policies

Terms and expectations for booking with art.pill TATTOO HOUSE.

Please review these before choosing a session and placing a deposit. Booking confirms that you understand the studio policies below.

Resource packet

For approved clients

These policies are linked from the private booking and confirmation emails so the practical details stay in one place.

Before booking

Review access, design, and fit.

  • Consultations are not mandatory, but they are available if needed in person or by video.
  • Clients must be at least 18 years old with valid ID. No exceptions.
  • Designs are shown on the day of the tattoo appointment unless a consultation has been requested, approved, and paid for.
  • Work is created in Saiel's own style. Exact copies or duplicates are not offered.
After booking

Deposits, timing, and day-of boundaries.

  • Clients have a 15-minute grace period. Arrival later than 15 minutes may require cancellation, rescheduling, and a new deposit.
  • Deposits are non-refundable and go toward the final tattoo cost.
  • One reschedule is allowed if requested at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Rescheduling within 48 hours requires a new deposit.
  • Final payment is due at the appointment.
  • Preferred payment is cash. Other options may include Cash App, Apple Pay, or credit/debit card.
  • Card transactions may include a processing fee.
  • Major last-minute design changes may require rescheduling and a new deposit.
  • No-shows forfeit the deposit and may not be rebooked.
  • Guests should be approved before the appointment.
  • The studio may refuse service if the client arrives visibly under the influence, unwell, or with active rashes, infections, or open wounds near the tattoo area.
