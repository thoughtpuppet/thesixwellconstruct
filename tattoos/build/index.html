Build Yours.

Instead of arriving with a folder of references That might not fully Communicate your idea or settling for a concept that has been repeated time and time again, you can draw from the visual language of saiel solehman's work itself. Choose meaningful symbols to build a brief, or draw your own maze, then submit it for review. The result is a design that speaks the same language as the paintings, the tattoos, and the clothing.

I

Enter the language

Read the symbols. Treat them like vocabulary words. Or, you can step into the maze studio. Each path draws from the same visual system.

II

Build or draw

Choose up to twelve elements that feel right, or compose a maze of your own. Either becomes your brief.

III

Submit for review

Your brief goes into review with placement, design intention, and timeline. If its feasible, a private token booking link follows. If not, I'll advise changes and we'll come to an agreement on what works best together.

There is an option to do this in-person at the art.pill Tattoo House studio. Learn more.
