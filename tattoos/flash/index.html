Claiming Flash

How It Works

Flash is booked through the same inquiry process as custom work. The difference is that the drawing already exists, so the review focuses on placement, size, timing, and whether the piece is still available.

One Claim

Most flash is tattooed once unless it is explicitly listed as repeatable. Claimed designs are removed or marked unavailable.

Fit Adjustments

Small changes for placement and scale are expected. Major concept changes move the project into custom work.

Deposit Required

Approved flash appointments require a deposit to hold the design and secure the session date.

Want to Claim a Piece?

Send the flash title, desired placement, approximate size, and your timeline. If the piece is available and the placement makes sense, you will receive the next booking step.
