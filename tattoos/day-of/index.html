Day-of prep

A practical checklist for arriving ready for your tattoo session.

Use this as your quick reference before coming in. If anything changes or needs attention, reply to the studio email thread before your appointment.

Session day

Come fed, rested, and on time.

The goal is a focused session with enough room for design review, placement, final payment, tattooing, and aftercare notes.

Bring

Valid ID and essentials

Bring a valid government-issued ID, any agreed reference notes, water, and anything you need for comfort during the session.

Wear

Easy access clothing

Wear clean, comfortable clothing that gives clear access to the tattoo area and can safely be marked or stained.

Prepare

Food, water, and rest

Eat a real meal before arriving, hydrate, and avoid coming in exhausted. Do not arrive under the influence.

Guests

Ask before bringing someone

Guests should be approved before the appointment. The studio may limit guests to protect the focus and safety of the session.

Design

Review happens at the appointment

Designs are shown on the day of the tattoo appointment unless a consultation has been requested, approved, and paid for.

Balance

Pay before tattooing begins

Your deposit goes toward the final tattoo cost. At the start of your appointment, after the final design, placement, and session price are confirmed, the remaining balance must be paid before tattooing begins. Cash is preferred; Cash App, Apple Pay, and credit/debit card are also accepted. A 3% processing fee applies to all digital transactions.

Arrival window

There is a 15-minute grace period. Arrival later than 15 minutes may require cancellation, rescheduling, and a new deposit.
