Owner / Artist

Saiel Dauhn Solehman

an Atlanta-based interdisciplinary artist whose visions move through tattooing, painting, film, apparel, and woodworking. Her practice explores mysticism, introspection, communication, psychological tension, and cultural systems through layered symbolism and visual conversations set within vivid, surreal environments.

How It Works

A review path before the session.

Every path begins with review. Flash claims, custom inquiries, and Special Projects are considered for fit before a private token booking link is sent.

I

Choose a COLLABORATION path

Claim available flash, submit a custom inquiry, build your own design online or in-person, or apply to participate in a  Special Project.

II

Project review

The request is reviewed for placement, scale, timing, concept, and fit.

III

Private booking

Approved clients receive a token booking link for scheduling and deposit.

IV

Session

The design is finalized with the body in mind, and the work begins.
