TATTOOS.

Original symbolic, surreal, and mythic tattooing. Guided by meaning. Designed to carry weight.

art.pill Tattoo House creates original, story-driven tattoos rooted in symbolism, mythology, and intentional design. Each piece is treated as a living artifact — built to carry memory, meaning, and personal mythology. Through collaboration, the client becomes part of the exchange: a meeting point between narrative, embodiment, and visual language, and a living window through which the work moves into the world.

The tattooing is the permanent mark-making node of the construct — where authorship, identity, and craft converge. It draws from the same image language as the paintings, the writings, and the archive, translated down to what a body can hold and carry across time.

How It Works

A review path before the session.

Every path begins with review. Flash claims, custom inquiries, and Special Projects are considered for fit before a private booking link is sent.
