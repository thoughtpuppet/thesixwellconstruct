the booking process

Every path begins with a review process. Flash claims, custom inquiries, and Special Projects are reviewed, then once approved, a  booking link is sent.

I

Choose a COLLABORATION path

Claim available flash, submit a custom inquiry, build your own design online or in-person, or apply to participate in a  Special Project.

II

Project review

The request is reviewed for placement, scale, timing, concept, and feasibility.

III

Private booking

Approved clients receive a booking link with a proposed session plan to schedule their session(s) and pay the deposit.

IV

Session

The design is finalized and the work begins.

    Full Day Session

    Extended Day Session

Quarter, Half, and Full Day sessions do not include the Extended Day fee, and your project may be split across shorter appointments if desired. If additional appointments are needed, I will coordinate the remaining dates with you.
