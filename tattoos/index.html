The Work

Original mythic, surreal, and symbolic tattooing

The practice is built around mythic figures, surreal scenes, symbolic architecture, emotionally loaded objects, abstract visual tension, and original flash that still feels authored and alive.

Mythic figures

Archetypal bodies and presences

Figures that feel ancient, ceremonial, protective, haunted, or transformed.

Surreal imagery

Dream logic over trend logic

Strange compositions, charged symbols, and visual tension that reward a longer look.

Original flash

Ready-made work with real authorship

Flash is treated as a finished creative offering, not filler between custom projects.

The Experience

A guided collaboration from concept to session

Working here means entering a paced creative process. The goal is not to rush you into an appointment, but to make sure the image, placement, scale, and emotional weight all land correctly.

Interpretation over replication

Strong projects leave room for visual authorship. References can guide the conversation, but the final design should feel like a tattoo with its own life.

Atmosphere matters

Sessions are treated like focused studio time: intentional pacing, honest communication, and enough space for the work to become what it needs to be.

Booking Process

Review first, booking second

  1. 01 Submit inquiry

    Share the idea, placement, scale, references, and timing through the inquiry route.

  2. 02 Project reviewed

    Each inquiry is screened for fit, scope, schedule, and alignment with the practice.

  3. 03 Approved clients get a link

    If the project is a match, you receive a private booking link and next-step notes.

  4. 04 Deposit secures the session

    The appointment becomes official once the deposit is placed through the booking flow.

  5. 05 Session happens

    The tattoo session is treated as the final stage of a process that has already been shaped well.
