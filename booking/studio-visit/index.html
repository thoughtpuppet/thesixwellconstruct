Studio Visit.

Spend time inside the art-making practice. An open studio visit is a scheduled window to see the space, the works in progress, finished paintings, and the process behind the construct.

Pick a time below. A deposit holds your visit; it can go toward anything you take home, or simply stand as your hold on the date.

What the visit is

  • A focused appointment for collectors, collaborators, friends of the practice, and people who want to meet the work in person.
  • Time to look through available paintings, active pieces, sketches, source material, and the studio environment without the pressure of an event.
  • A small visit by default. Bring one or two people if they are part of the conversation.

Before you arrive

The exact studio address is shared after the visit is confirmed. If you are hoping to see a specific work from the Art Making page, mention it in the note field so it can be ready.
