TATTOOS.

The ink & skin medium of Saiel Dauhn Solehman. Original, collaborative, and experimental works.

A private tattoo studio · Atlanta, Georgia

Art.Pill Tattoo House

Tattooing is one part of Saiel’s practice, in conversation with painting, film, clothing, symbols, and the people who carry the work. Each tattoo has to belong to its body and to the life it will live there.

The practice

A meeting point, not a template.

An idea arrives through a person. The work takes shape through meaning, conversation, drawing, placement, and the realities of skin.

Some projects begin with a memory or a question. Some begin with a symbol, an existing work, a piece of flash, or a form the client wants to explore. The first image is a direction—not a promise to reproduce a reference exactly.

I look for the composition that can hold that idea and still read clearly on your body. Scale, contrast, movement, surrounding tattoos, and the time the project needs are part of the artwork, not details added after it.

What we each bring

A collaboration with authorship.

You bring

Meaning & boundaries

Tell me what the subject means, what you respond to, where it might live, and which parts of your idea must remain. References can show a feeling or relationship without becoming a design to copy.

I shape

Form & translation

I develop composition, scale, visual language, contrast, and flow for the body. I may propose a different placement or simplify a detail so the tattoo can work as a tattoo.

We decide

The project plan

After review, we align on scope, budget, pacing, and the next step. A private booking link follows approval; the appointment is not reserved by sending an inquiry.

From idea to appointment

The process has room to think.

01

Choose a path

Flash, Custom, Build, or Special Projects. The proposed artist-led types are separate from limited calls. A consultation is available when planning needs a conversation first.

02

Project review

I review concept, body area, scale, references, timing, and fit. I may ask a follow-up question before deciding how to proceed.

03

Private booking

Approved clients receive a proposed session plan and reviewed budget before choosing an appointment and paying a deposit.

04

The session

We review the developed design and final placement in person, confirm the session price, and begin the work.
