Before you request

Artist-led design and deposit

Proposed language for this path. The 01 / Start Here terms and live Studio & Booking Policies ↗ remain the source of truth until a real offering is approved.

  • After I review your request, you receive the scope, budget, and likely session plan. An inquiry does not reserve a date or take a deposit.
  • If you choose to book, the deposit is paid before I develop the design. The current booking policy makes tattoo deposits non-refundable and credits them toward the tattoo.
  • You see the design at the appointment under the current policy. Minor refinements to the agreed direction are included; a substantially different concept or major last-minute change follows the current design-change and deposit policies.
  • You never have to be tattooed if you decide not to proceed. If the design fits the agreed type, named Anime source, and placement, simply not liking the artistic choices you left to me does not make the deposit refundable. A design that misses those agreed terms is a different issue.
