conceptual home vs commercial home

Thoughtpuppet artifacts are conceptually born in ART — painting-derived, painting-informed. But they sell through MERCH. The blue arrow marks that relationship: origin in ART, destination in MERCH. Two homes, different purposes.

how MERCH grows

Every medium that produces something sellable adds a commerce-flow arrow to MERCH. Flash prints from tattooing, zines from writings, releases from music — all route here. MERCH doesn't belong to any one medium. It belongs to the whole construct.

six.well clothing

Six.well clothing doesn't come from a specific medium — it comes from the construct itself (Saiel's own ideas, not informed by the paintings). Its flow arrow originates from the center medium.

page structure implication

On /merch, categories are organized by source medium, not by sub-brand. The filter reads: all · six.well · thoughtpuppet · tattooing flash · etc. Each category carries its source medium's color as a visual signal.
